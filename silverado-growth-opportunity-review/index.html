Structural Audit

6 Critical Findings

01
Keyword Quality

Broad Match Exposure & Relevance Drift

What's Happening
  • Broad match keywords are attracting high volumes of loosely related queries far outside senior living intent
  • Search term reports show spend consumed by irrelevant queries — diluting quality signals and raising cost per lead
Why It Matters
  • Every irrelevant click costs real budget and sends weak conversion signals to Smart Bidding
  • Relevance drift degrades Quality Score, raising CPCs across the entire account over time
Fix Migrate priority terms to exact/phrase, deploy tiered negative keyword lists, and implement monthly query audits
02
Targeting Architecture

Brand Demand Suppression via Account-Level Negatives

What's Happening
  • Entire countries have been excluded at the account level to suppress spam — the wrong lever for a geo-specific problem
  • This blunt approach can block legitimate in-market signals and suppress brand-adjacent queries from valid users
Why It Matters
  • The actual fix is server-level filtering or precise geo-targeting boundaries — not account-wide country exclusions
  • Blanket exclusions reduce addressable audience and create gaps in coverage near border markets
Fix Remove country-level negatives; implement defined inclusion zones per market with clean geo boundaries
03
Campaign Structure

Internal Campaign Cannibalization

What's Happening
  • St. Charles and multiple other markets have overlapping campaigns targeting the same geo footprint — causing self-competition in auction
  • Attribution is fragmented: conversions are split across duplicate campaigns making optimization signals unreliable
Why It Matters
  • Competing against yourself inflates CPCs and gives bidding algorithms conflicting signals
  • Leadership reporting on conversion volume is inaccurate — the same lead can be attributed to multiple campaigns
Fix Consolidate campaigns per market into single-ownership geo structures with clear hierarchy
04
Governance

Automation Governance Gaps

What's Happening
  • Auto-apply recommendations are enabled — the platform has been making structural changes without human review
  • No documented approval process exists for recommendation dismissal or acceptance across the portfolio
Why It Matters
  • Auto-applied recommendations have historically expanded match types and added broad keywords during budget cycles
  • Ungoverned automation compounds errors at scale — a single auto-applied change can affect 30 markets simultaneously
Fix Disable all auto-apply settings immediately; implement a weekly review protocol with documented decision log
05
Asset Policy

Policy-Restricted Delivery Limiting Competitiveness

What's Happening
  • Multiple asset groups across Performance Max campaigns show "Limited by policy" status — delivery is throttled
  • Restricted assets reduce the total eligible inventory pool, causing the campaign to under-serve available demand
Why It Matters
  • Policy-limited asset groups train bidding systems on constrained data, producing suboptimal bid strategies
  • Competitive share is lost to compliant advertisers whose delivery is unrestricted
Fix Audit all flagged assets, replace non-compliant creative, and submit reviews for assets that qualify for appeal
06
Geo Targeting

Geo Leakage & Reactive Exclusion Management

What's Happening
  • Campaigns are serving globally by default with exclusions applied reactively after the fact — evidenced by the location map
  • Red-shaded coverage across 6+ continents shows the exclusion approach is failing to contain delivery to relevant markets
Why It Matters
  • Budget is being consumed by users who will never visit or inquire about a Silverado community
  • Reactive exclusion is a lagging defense — waste occurs first, then gets partially corrected
Fix Flip to inclusion-first targeting: define radius or zip-code boundaries per community, remove global defaults
